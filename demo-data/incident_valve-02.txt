Incident Report: Valve-02 Seal Leak
Document ID: INC-2025-003
Date: 2025-01-05
Reported by: Operations Shift Supervisor

Asset: Valve-02
Location: Feed Line, Unit 1
Incident type: Process fluid leak

Description
A seal failure on Valve-02 resulted in a minor release of process fluid to the bund.
The asset was isolated and handed to maintenance within 30 minutes of detection.

Root cause analysis
Visual inspection confirmed degraded gland packing material. Seal sourced from
Flowserve; part reference FLO-GP-22. Packing had not been inspected at the
last preventive cycle (SOP-011 step 4 was skipped due to resource constraints).

Actions taken
1. Gland packing replaced (Flowserve FLO-GP-22).
2. Corrective action raised to update SOP-011 sign-off checklist.
3. Environmental authority notified per Factory Act Sec 36.

Related equipment: Heat Exchanger HE-01 (same feed line)
Governing procedure: SOP-011 (Cooling System Inspection)
Compliance flag: Factory Act Sec 36 - incident reporting obligation met.
