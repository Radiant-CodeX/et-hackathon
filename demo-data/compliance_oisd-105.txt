Compliance Reference: OISD-105 Work Permit System (Excerpt)
Authority: Oil Industry Safety Directorate

Key requirements for hot work permits:
1. A documented hazard identification must precede permit issue.
2. Gas testing must be performed and recorded before and during hot work.
3. A competent person must authorise the permit.
4. Manager sign-off is required prior to commencement in high-risk areas.
5. Permits must be closed and filed on completion.

Related procedures: Hot Work Permit Procedure (HWP-002)
Applicable areas: all process units handling hydrocarbons.
