Date: 2024-07-10

Asset: Pump-01

Inspection scope
Full strip-down inspection per PESO Pressure Vessel Rules and OEM manual.

Findings
1. Impeller: minor erosion on leading edge; within acceptable limits. No action.
2. Mechanical seal: slight wear; replaced as precaution (Flowserve FLO-MS-11).
3. Bearing housing: fretting corrosion visible on DE bearing seat — significant.
   Previous bearing replacement appears to have used an undersized fit.
4. Shaft: within tolerance. No straightness deviation detected.

Recommendations
- Bearing housing to be re-bored to correct tolerance before next bearing fit.
- Confirm whether SKF #6309 is the correct bearing specification for this application
  (OEM manual specifies #6308; discrepancy requires engineering review).
- Schedule follow-up inspection in 6 months.
