Procedure: Hot Work Permit Procedure
Document ID: HWP-002
Revision: 3
Governed by: OISD-105 (Work Permit System)

Purpose
This procedure governs the issue of hot work permits for welding, cutting,
and grinding activities in process areas.

Steps
1. Requestor completes the hot work permit form.
2. Area is inspected for flammable materials and gas accumulation.
3. Gas test is performed and recorded.
4. Fire watch is assigned for the duration of the work.
5. Work proceeds; permit is closed on completion.

Equipment in scope: Pump-01, Heat-Exchanger-A2

Note: This revision does not include a manager sign-off checkpoint prior to
commencement of hot work.
