Procedure: Cooling System Inspection
Document ID: SOP-011
Revision: 2
Governed by: PESO Pressure Vessel Rules 2023

Purpose
Define the routine inspection steps for all cooling-water-loop assets:
Heat Exchanger HE-01, Pump-01, and associated valves.

Steps
1. Isolate cooling water supply and confirm zero pressure.
2. Drain and visually inspect tube bundle for fouling or corrosion.
3. Record shell pressure rating against PESO certification.
4. Inspect gland packing on all valves in the loop (including Valve-02).
5. Restore cooling water supply; confirm no leaks.
6. File completed checklist in the plant document management system.

Equipment in scope: Heat Exchanger HE-01, Pump-01, Valve-02
Related compliance: PESO-2023 (annual inspection certificate required)
